# Privacy policy — Déclic

*Last updated: 14 November 2026. Translation of the [French version](confidentialite.md), which prevails.*

## In short

Your data stays on your phone. We don't receive it, we don't see it, we don't sell it.
No account, no ads, no trackers, no audience measurement.

## Controller

alexandremarce3 (non-professional publisher) — contact@lenspicture.com

## Data concerned

- Your usage profile (photographer / model) and your age declaration;
- your identity if you fill it in (nickname, first name, last name — all optional);
- your progress (lessons, challenges, XP, achievements, avatar);
- the photos you add to the journal;
- your collaboration agreements (Quick Agreement) and your moodboard.

## Where is it stored?

Only in the app's private storage, on your phone. None of it is sent to the publisher or to any
third party. Journal photos are copied into a folder reserved for the app, which other apps cannot
access. Android automatic backup (to Google Drive) is disabled for Déclic: only a backup you export
yourself lets you recover your data.

## What is it used for?

Only to make the app work: show your content, compute your XP and ranks, pre-fill your agreements.
Nothing else.

## Phone permissions

The camera is only requested when you take a photo for the journal. For the gallery, Déclic uses the
phone's photo picker: the app only receives the photos you choose, never access to your whole library.
The microphone is never used.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared automatically. When you export a backup or an agreement as a PDF, you choose where to send it.

## Retention

As long as the app is installed, or until you erase your data. Uninstalling the app erases everything.

## Your rights

- **Erasure**: Profile > "Erase all my data", in one tap, permanently.
- **Portability**: Profile > Backup > Export.
- **Access and rectification**: everything is visible and editable in the app.

For any question, write to the address above. You can also contact the French data protection
authority, the CNIL ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
